Privacy Policy

ProjectGuard

An offline-first financial-integrity app for Ghanaian construction contractors. This page explains what data the app handles, where it goes, and what choices you have.

Effective date: 29 May 2026  ·  Version: 2.0

The short version

Your financial records live on your phone. Almost nothing leaves it. The exceptions are clearly named below: sign-in, optional cloud backup to your own Google Drive, and — only if you choose to switch it on — the Charley AI assistant, which can read selected slices of your data to answer questions.

2. What data is collected, and where it lives

2.1 On your phone (does not leave the device by default)

  • Financial entries you create: transactions, projects, BOQ items, phase budgets, invoices, loans, exchange rates.
  • Receipt photos you take through the in-app scanner, stored in the app's private documents directory.
  • SMS alerts ingested by the app: the sender, the message body, the parsed amount, direction (credit/debit), and the detected account/wallet (MTN MoMo, AT Money, Telecel Cash, bank).
  • Notebook entries, checklists and project memos.
  • App preferences (theme, app-lock state, onboarding state, Charley consent flag).
  • Charley conversation history (if you've turned Charley on).

2.2 Sign-in & access (Google + Firebase)

When you sign in with Google, the app receives your email and display name. We use Firebase services (Google LLC) for:

  • Firebase Authentication — to identify your account and bind it to this device.
  • Firestore — to store a small access record: your email, your role (admin / contractor / accountant / site manager / personal assistant), the device that claimed your account, the time you last signed in, and (for QR-invited team members) the invite that linked you. No financial figures are written to Firestore.
  • Firebase Remote Config — to deliver runtime settings (e.g. the AI provider keys and feature flags) without an app update.
  • Firebase Crashlytics — if a crash occurs, an anonymised crash report (stack trace, device model, OS version) is sent so we can fix it. Your financial data is never included in a crash report.

2.3 Backups (your Google Drive, optional)

If you turn on backup, the app writes an encrypted snapshot of your local database into the AppData folder of your own Google Drive. The AppData folder is a hidden area only this app can read; even you cannot see it through the normal Drive UI. Nothing is sent to our servers; your data stays inside your Google account.

3. The Charley AI assistant (opt-in)

Charley is the in-app AI helper. It is off by default. To use it you must explicitly turn it on; you can turn it off again any time from More → Settings → Charley AI assistant or from the menu inside the chat. With Charley off, no question and no figures leave your phone for AI processing.

3.1 What gets sent when Charley is on

When you ask Charley a question (in the floating mini-chat or the full chat), the app sends, over HTTPS, to one of the AI providers below:

  • The text of your question.
  • Only the slice of figures Charley needs to answer it — for example a treasury snapshot when you ask about cash, a project's totals when you ask about a project, or a week summary when you ask about spending. Charley does not upload your whole database.
  • If you ask Charley to take an action (record an expense, draft a letter, create a project), the parameters of that action — the app then performs the write locally on your phone after you confirm.

3.2 AI providers used

ProviderModelRoleTraining note

Requests fall through to the next provider only when the previous one is unavailable or rate-limited. We do not pick which provider handles a given question — the failover order is deterministic.

3.3 Smart SMS classification (opt-in & online only)

The app's regex-based SMS parser runs entirely on your phone and identifies almost all real bank / mobile-money alerts without any network call. When Charley is on and you open a single message on the SMS Review screen, the app additionally sends the message's sender and body to one of the AI providers above so Charley can read the message in context — for example, to recognise that a 1-cedi credit is a loyalty rebate, not a regular transfer. This per-message context pass is the only time the SMS body is sent to a cloud service; with Charley off, the parser works fully on-device.

3.4 Hybrid receipt OCR (opt-in & online only)

Receipt photos are read by Google ML Kit on your phone by default. If Charley is on, you are online, and the on-device read is inconclusive (blurred image, unusual layout), the app silently sends a downscaled copy of the receipt image to Google Gemini to extract the total and vendor. The original photo always stays on your phone.

3.5 Bring your own AI key

The "Use your own AI key" feature, when enabled by the developer through Remote Config, lets you paste a free Groq, Gemini or Cerebras key of your own into the app settings. When a key is set, it is stored only on your phone and used instead of the app's shared key — so your Charley requests run on your personal quota, not the shared one. You can clear the key any time to return to the shared service.

5. Data retention & deletion

  • On-device data stays until you uninstall the app or clear its storage in Android settings.
  • Drive backups live in your own Drive AppData folder. You can revoke this app's Drive access from your Google account at any time, which orphans the backup file; you can also delete it through Drive Apps & Web management.
  • Firestore access record persists while your account is active. If your account is revoked from the whitelist, the record's status is set to revoked. You can request full deletion by emailing us.
  • Crash reports follow Google's Crashlytics retention (typically 90 days).
  • Charley conversation logs live on your phone only — the AI providers do not retain them on our behalf. Delete a conversation from the chat's history menu and it's gone.

6. Security

  • All network traffic uses HTTPS / TLS.
  • The local database is stored inside the app's private sandbox, which other apps on the device cannot read.
  • Optional app lock with a 4-digit PIN and optional biometric unlock — the app re-locks whenever it returns from the background.
  • Sign-in is tied to a device fingerprint so a stolen credential alone can't open your account on another phone.

7. Children

ProjectGuard is intended for use by adult construction contractors and their team members. It is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from them.

8. Your rights and choices

  • Turn Charley off any time — More → Settings → Charley. With Charley off, nothing financial leaves your phone for AI processing.
  • Revoke Android permissions (SMS, camera, storage, notifications) from Android Settings → Apps → ProjectGuard → Permissions. The corresponding features stop working but the rest of the app continues.
  • Revoke Drive backup via your Google account's "Apps with access" page.
  • Export or delete your data — email us at projectguard101@gmail.com and we'll help. On-device data you can also clear directly by uninstalling the app or clearing its storage.
  • Bring your own AI key — when this feature is enabled, you can move your Charley usage entirely onto your own quota by pasting a personal key in More → Settings → Your own AI key.
